\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Protected adaptation and permanent raw release require different security
evidence.  Runtime assurance can keep the executed composition safe while
adversarial reward provenance changes the policy that later receives action
authority.  Carr et al.'s shield-retirement workflow provides an independent
lifecycle instance of this transition.  Bootstrap-only poisoning increased
raw-policy violations at retirement by factors of $3.6$ and $1.6$ in two of
three provenance-complete paired REINFORCE training runs.  The freshly
regenerated third pair was unchanged, and protected execution remained
violation-free.

The controlled Safe-Control-Gym study isolated the same evidence boundary.
After the attack and five-step release check were fixed, poisoned permanent
release failed on 27/120 snapshot--state pairs, compared with 2/120 for clean
release.  Resident predictive authority incurred 0/120 failures and switched
before every corresponding poisoned failure.  A one-step resident kernel
still failed on 14/71 accepted pairs, showing that repeated recoverability
checks, rather than residence alone, supplied the observed protection.

Exploitability remained conditional on the training realization.  Across 50
locked obstacle pairs, all 11 positive outcomes had clean retirement-state
disagreement below $0.06$, and none of 32 high-disagreement runs was positive.
Seven of 18 low-disagreement corpus runs were non-positive.  The separate
fresh REINFORCE pair was also low-disagreement and non-positive.  Low
disagreement therefore identifies an opportunity region without predicting
attack success.  Because the measurement is taken at retirement, it
characterizes the realized state rather than an online attacker-side trigger.

The resulting design rule is contract-specific.  Provenance binding and
trusted recomputation close the upstream reward-record channel.  Commit
admission constrains which raw snapshot can receive authority, while resident
predictive authority and permanent filtering contain downstream physical
consequences.  Evidence collected for an assured composition remains bound to
its subject, authority, provenance, information, and horizon.  Raw release
must re-establish the obligations changed by the authority transition.
