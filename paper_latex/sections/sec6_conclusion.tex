\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A five-step raw-policy test did not support the evaluated 120-step claim, and
raw-policy warning did not establish LQR recovery after transfer.  The clean
cohort observed 10/480 raw failures across five of 20 controllers and none
under repeated lookahead.  Under constrained poisoning, 19/116 pairs failed
only under raw execution and 7/116 only after the LQR handoff.  The third-party
attack increased retirement violations in two of three provenance-complete
pairs, but was conditional across training runs.  Trusted recomputation closes
the reward-record channel when applicable; otherwise data integrity requires
separate controls.  Independently, safety evidence must match each receiving
controller and deployment horizon.
