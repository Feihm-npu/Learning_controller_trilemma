\section{Deployment Contracts and Escape Conditions}
\label{sec:lifecyclegate}

LifecycleGate is a contract-enforcement layer around a severe-attack-aware
filter.  It consumes the certified kernel produced by an invariant, barrier,
reachability, or shield certificate.  The layer then accepts, projects, or
rejects a proposed learning update.

\textbf{Update-admission interface.}
At history $h_t$, an estimator supplies $X_{\mathcal A}(h_t)$, a certificate
module supplies $K_\Gamma(h_t)$, and the learner proposes
$\theta^+=U(\theta_t,h_t)$.  The prototype checks the current action against
$K_\Gamma$ or, when a future-history abstraction is available, checks or
projects the full update against $\Theta_\Gamma$.  For linear policies this is
the convex halfspace system in Proposition~\ref{prop:parameter-gate}.  A grid
certifies only its listed points unless the cover, regularity, and model-error
premises of Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-lift} justify its robust margin.

\textbf{Contract choices.}
The mechanism can accept, project, freeze, roll back, or request a trusted
anchor that shrinks $X_{\mathcal A}$.  Commit admission certifies a candidate
on a declared state set and horizon.  It backtracks along the connected segment
from the last admitted snapshot to exclude disconnected safe islands.  A
permanent filter certifies $F\circ\pi_{\theta^+}$ at runtime.  Resident
predictive authority repeatedly tests recoverability and switches to the
baseline when required.  Commit admission incurs coverage and model-mismatch
risk; resident mechanisms incur intervention, rejection, and online cost.

\textbf{Fail-closed semantics.}
An empty plausible-state kernel, an uncertified snapshot, model failure, or
solver failure triggers a fail-closed response.  The gate freezes or rolls
back and reports the event.  A trusted anchor can resume learning by changing
the justified plausible-state set and carries an explicit infrastructure cost.

\textbf{Utility is an independent property.}
The gate enforces certificate membership independently of the task objective.
A safe update may be neutral or harmful.  Accepted parameter distance measures
update availability, whereas paired task outcomes against always-freeze measure
learning utility under benign change.
