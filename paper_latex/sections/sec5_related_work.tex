\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Runtime assurance and safe controller updates.}
Simplex architectures retain a decision module and a trusted baseline during
online upgrades~\cite{seto_simplex_1998}; neural and barrier-based Simplex
extend this with online retraining, reverse switching, and switching
conditions under bounded model error
\cite{phan_neural_simplex_2020,damare_bb_simplex_2025}; and model-predictive
safety certification (MPSC) and runtime shields preserve recoverability or
prevent unsafe actions \cite{wabersich_linear_mpsc_2018,
didier_adaptive_mpsc_2021,alshiekh_shielding_2018}.  All of these certify a
resident composition, whereas we consider a transition in which the raw policy
later receives authority.  Policy repair, safe projection, joint
controller/certificate repair, robust conformal transfer, and shielded
sim-to-real transfer instead establish evidence for the deployment object
before use \cite{zhou_runtime_policy_repair_2020,lu_repair_preservation_2024,
chow_safe_policy_2021,yu_certificate_repair_2025,
mirzaeedodangeh_safe_updates_2026,hsu_sim_lab_real_2023}.

\textbf{Shield retirement and attacked state semantics.}
Carr et al.~\cite{carr_partial_shielding_2023} study a partially observable
bootstrap-to-retirement lifecycle and compare sudden with smooth shield
removal under trusted learning signals.  We reuse that published lifecycle as
a third-party case study and add bounded reward-record poisoning during the
protected bootstrap; the resulting experiment concerns evidence reuse across
the same authority transition, not the soundness of their original claims.

Secure estimation characterizes when sensor attacks can be detected or
corrected~\cite{pasqualetti_attack_2013,fawzi_secure_2014}, and severe-attack
CBF filtering constructs plausible-state sets and safe-action kernels when the
state cannot be reconstructed uniquely~\cite{tan_safety_2024}.  Our model
consumes such a set and asks whether an adversarially updated action map
remains inside its certified kernel.  Related lines learn barriers, certify
reinforcement-learning robustness, or solve adversarial reach--avoid problems
under trusted learning signals \cite{brunke_barrier_learning_2022,
capone_bandit_safe_control_2025,wu_certifying_2022,pham_certified_2024,
hsu_isaacs_2023,nguyen_gameplay_2024}.

\textbf{Policy and reward poisoning.}
Policy poisoning has been studied in batch reinforcement learning and
control~\cite{ma_policy_poisoning_2019}; online attacks perturb individual
rewards under an $\ell_\infty$ budget and adapt to the learning
process~\cite{zhang_reward_poisoning_2020}; and extensions cover
policy-gradient learners without known dynamics and black-box deep
reinforcement learning, and targeted poisoning can teach a chosen policy
\cite{sun_online_rl_poisoning_2021,xu_deep_reward_poisoning_2025,
rakhsha_policy_teaching_2020,everitt_corrupted_reward_2017}.  None of this
line places the learner behind a runtime shield.

BADControl poisons operational data in PID and LQR loops to implant a
vulnerability activated by a physical trigger~\cite{burbano_badcontrol_2026}.
Our contribution concerns a different composition: bounded reward-record
poisoning affects a policy trained behind runtime assurance, and failure
appears after authority retirement without an activation input.  Where online
reward-poisoning defenses model adaptive attacker--defender interaction
\cite{nika_reward_defense_2023}, we separate provenance, trusted
recomputation, detection, commit admission, and runtime containment.

\section{Discussion and Limitations}

The evidence supports an architectural vulnerability with conditional
exploitability.  Protected adaptation certifies the executed composition while
resident authority remains active; permanent raw release changes the subject,
authority, provenance, information, and horizon of that evidence.  Neither
result requires the shield or filter itself to be unsound, and the clean
cohort fixes the ordering: the transition is a contract gap before it is an
attack surface, and the upstream channel changes the rate of post-release
failure rather than creating its possibility.

The attack has a structured opportunity region that does not determine
success: every positive outcome in the locked corpus occurred at low
retirement-state disagreement and no high-disagreement run was positive, yet
the fresh REINFORCE pair, seven low-band corpus runs, and the SAC battery show
that headroom is insufficient for the fixed attack.  The defenses separate
integrity from authority.  Provenance binding and trusted reward recomputation
remove the evaluated upstream channel while leaving the contract gap in place;
task-aware checks detect the effective fixed attack under additional semantic
assumptions; commit admission limits which raw snapshot receives authority;
and resident predictive authority contains unsafe proposals at runtime, where
the one-step kernel shows that residence alone is insufficient and the
decision rule must preserve recoverability over the relevant horizon.  These
contracts incur different coverage, intervention, latency, and reward costs,
so the choice is an explicit architectural tradeoff rather than a default.

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\emph{The marker is descriptive, not an attacker trigger.}  Retirement-state
disagreement is observed after the protected bootstrap and therefore
characterizes a realized deployment state.  An attacker-side trigger would
require evidence that earlier disagreement trajectories predict the eventual
opportunity region.  The reported AUC is consequently a descriptive measure
over the locked corpus rather than an externally validated classifier, and the
marker is obstacle-scoped: the second environment also contained protective
outcomes at high clean disagreement.

\emph{The formal results have bounded verification scope.}  The current- and
future-history gates state certificate obligations, and the linear parameter
gate is exact on its finite abstraction; the continuous lift additionally
requires a justified reachable-set cover, regularity and model-error bounds,
and a robust margin, which the coverage audit probes without establishing
continuous-state invariance.  The exact reward-influence result covers
a fixed standardized-advantage batch with global gradient clipping, leaving
coordinatewise parameter clipping and multi-batch attack construction outside
its scope.

\emph{The empirical generality is bounded.}  The end-to-end physical effect is
established for low-dimensional Monte Carlo policy-gradient learners.  The
learned-baseline variant shares the same return and attack channel rather than
forming an independent learner family.  The official Safe-Control-Gym PPO
audit showed checkpoint-level redirection without stable physical failure.  On
the third-party lifecycle, PPO and SAC contained individual positive training
realizations but did not provide reliable success across paired runs.

\emph{The threat model starts after access is obtained.}  It does not estimate
the prevalence of bounded reward-ingestion access across products, and it does
not model service exploitation.  The effective fixed attack is detectable by
the tested task-aware checks, and the sign-respecting variants did not retain
deployment harm.  The trusted-anchor audit models bounded error only for altitude and pitch and
reports calls rather than hardware-specific cost.  These boundaries limit claims about
stealth, cross-architecture prevalence, and deployment overhead without
changing the authority-transition result.
